Our story

Fire, spice and a seat at the table

ILE Lagos Chophouse was built around one idea: that great Nigerian cooking deserves a room as bold as its flavours. Come hungry, stay for the evening.

Where it began

ILE means "home" in Yoruba, and that is how we want every guest to feel. Our kitchen is built around open-fire grilling, slow-simmered stews and the spice blends that Lagos grew up on.

We cook with the same care you would find at a family table: fresh peppers ground daily, meats seasoned overnight, and sauces that are never rushed.
